Heel while reaching showed a +5.9° signed imbalance — on the same point of sail where the reach percentage cratered to 40.6%; the two appearing together is worth investigating, though this data alone doesn't establish that the heel imbalance caused the reach shortfall. The two clearest strengths were downwind performance (near-optimal angle) and upwind percentage holding level with the comparable race; the two most important gaps were the reach collapse and tack execution.
