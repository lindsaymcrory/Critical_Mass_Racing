Heel while beating averaged 18.3° with a clear positive imbalance (+5.9° signed) — the largest signed heel skew recorded so far, which could reflect more time on one tack, uneven crew weight, or a rig asymmetry; the data can't distinguish between those. The two clearest strengths were downwind performance (the best run result recorded) and tight downwind angle discipline; the two most important gaps were the recurring upwind angle deviation and the beat heel imbalance.
