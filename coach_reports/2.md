Heel while beating averaged 20.3° with almost no side imbalance (+0.4° signed) — high but slightly less than race 1's 23.5°, broadly consistent with the similarly strong breeze. The two clearest strengths were low gybe loss (11.5%, the best on record) and tighter upwind angle discipline than the comparable race; the two most important gaps were downwind angle (sailing too deep, opposite of the comparable race) and slower tack recovery.
