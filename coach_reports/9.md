Overall the boat achieved 71.5% of polar target upwind but only 62.3% downwind, the latter well below race 5's 79.1%. Four mark roundings were logged, still too few for reliable leg-by-leg splits. The Vakaros line was never pinged this race, so no distance-to-line figure is available; boat speed just after the gun was approximately 4.5 kn. Heel while running averaged just 1.0° — unusually flat, alongside the very deep average run angle noted above; the two appearing together is consistent with sailing close to dead downwind for much of the run, though the data doesn't confirm cause and effect. The two clearest strengths were upwind speed as a share of target and fast tack recovery; the two most important gaps were the large downwind angle deviation and the complete absence of gybes in a race with substantial run time.
