## True wind conditions

* TWS ranged from **0.9 to 12.5 kn** (avg **7.3 kn**; middle 90% was 4.5–9.8 kn).
* Direction veered from ~105–108° at the start to a steadier 136–163° through most of the race (circular std 20°, mean 149°) — a real early shift of roughly 30–50°, settling down after the first 30–40 minutes.
* Wind dipped light early (5.1–5.6 kn, 20:03–20:30), built to a mid-race peak of 9.3 kn (21:13), then died away completely in the final 15 minutes (down to 1.8 kn at the finish) — a classic dying evening breeze.

## Tacks and gybes

* **8 tacks**, avg duration **8.4 s**, avg speed loss **34.0%**, avg recovery **11.4 s** (the slowest average recovery of any race reviewed).
* Best tack: **21:50:15** — 6 s, tight 73° swing, only **25.3%** loss — though recovery afterward still took 18 s, showing loss% and recovery speed don't always move together.
* Two tacks (20:15:13 and 20:15:23) happened only 10 seconds apart — likely a double/aborted tack rather than two independent maneuvers; the first shows an unusually short 2 s duration with almost no speed loss, which looks like a data artifact of that sequence rather than a real clean tack.
* **8 gybes**, avg duration **10.2 s**, avg speed loss **23.2%** — notably *better* on average than this race's own tacks (34.0%). **Observation:** gybe execution outperformed tack execution this race, worth understanding why since usually the reverse is assumed.

## Upwind performance

* Beat boat speed averaged **67.5% of polar target** (3,724 samples, avg TWS 7.5 kn).
* Average TWA was **42.8°**, almost exactly matching the polar-optimal **41.8°** at this wind speed — the closest angle match of any race reviewed so far.
* Actual VMG averaged **2.69 kn** vs. a polar target of **3.75 kn** — **71.7% of target VMG**, slightly better than the 67.5% boat-speed figure (consistent with the angle being essentially spot-on).
* **Likely explanation:** with angle this close to optimal, the shortfall from target is almost entirely a raw boat-speed issue, not a pointing issue.

## Power and boat handling

* Average heel while beating was **11.4°**, scaling sensibly with the lighter 7.5 kn average wind (between the 8.1° seen at 8.2 kn and 13.6° seen at 9.2 kn in other races).
* Rate of turn upwind averaged **1.01°/s** — the calmest steering of any race reviewed, consistent with the generally light, settled conditions.
* **Data insufficient:** no traveler/vang/rudder data available to assess power management directly.

## Boat trim

* Heel scaled down by point of sail as expected; downwind (run) performance was notably lower this race (57.4% of target) than any other race reviewed, though the dataset doesn't include a dedicated downwind-performance section here to explore why — worth a look given how much of the race (3,718 samples) was spent running.

## Comparison with other races

* Four other races were sailed in broadly similar (light-to-moderate) wind: Race IDs 4 (8.2 kn beat avg), 5 (9.2 kn), 7 (7.0 kn), and 8 (6.5 kn).
* This race's upwind angle (42.8° vs. 41.8° target) was the **closest match to polar-optimal** of any race reviewed to date — tighter than Race 4 (37.4° vs. 41.1°, slightly under target) and much tighter than Race 5 (47.4° vs. 40.1°, well over target).
* Tack execution (34.0% avg loss) was mid-pack for the group — better than Races 4 (36.9%) and 5 (35.6%), but well behind Races 7 (23.5%) and 8 (31.8%).

## Coaching priorities

1. **Downwind (run) performance (medium confidence).** Run boat speed was only 57.4% of target — the worst of any race reviewed — over a large chunk of this race (3,718 samples). *Action:* review downwind trim and technique specifically for this night, since it stands out from every other comparable race.
2. **Tack vs. gybe execution gap (medium confidence).** Gybes averaged 23.2% speed loss, tacks 34.0% — an unusual reversal from what's typically expected. *Action:* worth discussing with the crew what was different about tack execution that night.
3. **Tack recovery time (low confidence).** Average recovery was 11.4 s, the slowest of any race reviewed, even on tacks with modest speed loss (e.g. the 25.3%-loss tack at 21:50 still took 18 s to recover). *Action:* drill the acceleration phase specifically, as with other races showing this pattern.
