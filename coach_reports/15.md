### Coaching Recommendations

1. Reach has now been the weakest point of sail in four separate light-air races (38.8% here, similarly weak in races 8, 12, and 13) — this is a confirmed pattern, not a one-off; prioritize dedicated light-air reach-mode testing.
2. Both upwind (5.2° wide) and downwind (6.1° shy) angles missed target in this light breeze — work on angle discipline specifically when the wind is fading, rather than defaulting to one "footing" mode regardless of point of sail.
3. The last tack of the night (22:48, in the extended race window) lost 36.6% of speed, the highest loss of the race — worth reviewing execution late in a long, light race when focus may be flagging.
